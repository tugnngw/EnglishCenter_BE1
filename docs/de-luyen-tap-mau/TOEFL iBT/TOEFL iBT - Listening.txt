1. What is the lecture mainly about?
*A. How honeybees communicate the location of food
B. How honeybees make honey
C. The life cycle of a honeybee colony
D. Threats to honeybee populations

*A. The direction of the food compared to the sun
B. The number of flowers at the food source
C. The quality of the nectar
D. The position of the queen bee
3. What does a longer waggle tell the other bees?
A. There is more food.
*B. The food is farther away.
C. There is danger near the hive.

4. Who was Karl von Frisch?
A. A beekeeper who trained bees to dance
*B. A scientist who explained the meaning of the waggle dance
C. A student who disagreed with the professor
D. A photographer who filmed bees
5. What does the professor imply about recent research?
A. The waggle dance is not important.
B. Bees cannot see the sun.
*C. Bees use smell in addition to the dance.
D. Von Frisch's work was completely wrong.
