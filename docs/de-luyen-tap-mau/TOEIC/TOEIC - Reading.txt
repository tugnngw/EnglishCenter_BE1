Subject: Office renovation
Dear colleagues,
Please note that the third floor will be closed from Monday, 10 March, to Friday, 21 March, while new carpets and lighting are installed. During this period, the sales and marketing teams will work in the training rooms on the first floor. Parking will not be affected.
Please take all personal items home or place them in the boxes provided by Thursday, 6 March. The IT department will move your computers over the weekend, so you do not need to do anything with your equipment.
If you have any questions, please contact me at extension 214.
Thank you for your cooperation.
Daniel Park
