[PASSAGE] Young Volunteers
In recent years, more and more young people in Vietnam have joined volunteer activities. Some teach English to children in remote villages, while others clean beaches, plant trees or help elderly people in their neighbourhood. According to a survey by a youth organisation, nearly 40 percent of university students took part in at least one volunteer project last year.
There are many reasons for this trend. Firstly, social media makes it easy to find projects and to share experiences with friends. Secondly, many students believe that volunteering helps them develop skills that are not taught in the classroom, such as teamwork, communication and problem solving. Employers also value these skills; some companies even ask about volunteer experience during job interviews.
However, volunteering is not always easy. Students often have to balance their studies, part-time jobs and volunteer work. Some projects also require travel to faraway places, which can be tiring and expensive. Experts suggest that students should start with short local projects before joining longer ones.
Overall, volunteering gives young people a chance to help their communities while also preparing for their future careers.
